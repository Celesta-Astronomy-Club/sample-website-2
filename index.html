Astronomy, Technology
and Access

Celesta is an astronomy-focused student organization built around education, research, technical projects, outreach and hands-on STEM learning.

Join the Discord About Celesta

At its core, Celesta aims to make astronomy more approachable without reducing its intellectual depth. Students are encouraged not only to learn about astronomy, but to use data, software, research methods and engineering to explore it.

Beyond the classroom

Opportunities and educational material for students who want more than a conventional classroom offers.

  • Education

    Astronomy and astrophysics content, online workshops and guest sessions.

  • Research

    Projects built around real scientific datasets.

  • Technology

    Technical and software-based STEM projects.

  • Competitions

    Student competitions and hackathons.

  • Mentorship

    Mentorship and peer learning between students.

  • Internships

    Students contribute to technology, research, social media and organizational development, with interns joining from countries including Tunisia and Brazil.

  • Outreach

    Astronomy outreach through social media and digital platforms.

Learn astronomy through conversation

CelestaGPT is an AI-based astronomy mentor developed as part of Celesta's educational initiatives. It helps students explore astronomy and related STEM topics through conversational guidance.
